# Getting Started

Mission Scheduler is free, hosted software. Getting your organization on it looks like this:

## 1. Reach out
Fill in the form at https://missionscheduler.com — organization name, contact details, and roughly
how many pilots and missions per month you run. We'll set up a short conversation.

## 2. We set you up
We create your organization on the hosted service and your coordinator's account. You'll sign in at
**app.missionscheduler.com** (organizations can also get their own subdomain). Your templates,
settings, and an "All Pilots" group come pre-seeded; your data is isolated and visible only to
your organization.

## 3. Your coordinator builds the roster
From the web app: add your destinations (the airports you fly to), add your pilots (each gets a
login and can set availability flags like IFR or night), and add your passengers.

## 4. Fly
Post a mission, invite your pilots (everyone or a group), and the first pilot to accept gets it —
everyone is notified automatically by email, text, and push. Pilots can also add passengers and
book their own missions; offering a mission to other pilots goes through your coordinator's
approval.

## 5. Phones
Pilots install the app straight from the browser — iPhone (Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen)
or Android (Chrome → Install). No app store, nothing to update.

## Questions?
Ask in [Discussions → Q&A](../../../discussions). Support is community-based; there's no phone or
email queue, but questions get read.
