# Mission Scheduler — Community

**Mission Scheduler** is a free flight scheduler and pilot manager for public benefit flying
organizations — volunteer medical and compassion flight groups, animal rescue flight networks,
disaster-relief flying, and flying clubs that run community missions.

It was built by **Mercy Flights on Orcas Island, Washington**, a volunteer group that flies island
residents to mainland medical appointments at no charge, and is given away to similar organizations
at no cost.

**Website:** https://missionscheduler.com
**Get Mission Scheduler:** https://missionscheduler.com/#get — the software is free; each
organization receives its own private copy (own database, own domain, own mobile app builds) after
a short conversation.

## What it does

- **Missions** — request, invite, assign, confirm, relinquish; the full lifecycle with every step
  notified by email, SMS, and push.
- **Pilots** — profiles, aircraft type, availability flags, groups (IFR, night, weekdays…), and
  one-click broadcasts to all pilots or a group.
- **Passengers** — records, contact preferences, and a passenger portal.
- **Calendar** — month, week, day, and list views.
- **Pilot self-service** — pilots can add passengers and book their own missions; offering a
  mission to other pilots goes through admin approval.
- **Mobile app** — iOS and Android for pilots: accept or decline missions from your phone.
- **Notifications** — every message template is editable by your admin, with shortcodes.
- **Branding** — your logo, colors, and login imagery.
- **Reports** — pilot mission history and exports on every grid.
